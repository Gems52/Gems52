### 🛰️ About

Cybersecurity practitioner going by **GEMS52**. Every system tells a story, I read it like an attacker and rewrite it like a defender.

I'm the **admin of RUY**, a highly competitive international CTF team, and the **captain of WOLFSKIN**, a national team. Competing at this level taught me to work under pressure, split problems fast, and lead people toward results.

My approach is simple: **attack to understand, defend to protect.** I document what I learn in clear, reproducible writeups, and every one ends with remediation advice, because you only truly know how to attack when you know how to defend.

**Open to opportunities:** internships (on-site or remote), collaborations and freelance missions in cybersecurity. Let's build something secure.
